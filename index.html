Our Story

More than a game —
it's a vibe.

Boru started as a dream: a place where people could unwind, connect, and play without the formality. Nestled in MaryCris Complex, General Trias, we've become the neighborhood's favorite late-night destination.

Managed by a warm family team, we pour heart into every cup and every table. Whether you're here for a quick coffee, an intense billiards match, or an all-night PS5 session — you're family here.

We're proud of our 4.9 Google rating, but what we're really proud of is the community we've built — barkada nights, family bondings, and strangers who leave as friends.
